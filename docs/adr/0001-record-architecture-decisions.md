# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

**Status:** Accepted (26 November 2026)
**Source:** R-03 in [../engineering/recommendations.md](../engineering/recommendations.md)

## Context
Decisions were made in conversation and in chat-based registers, with no durable record in the codebase.

## Decision
Each significant decision is recorded as a short ADR in `docs/adr/`, numbered in order. An ADR is never edited after acceptance except to mark it **Superseded by ADR-nnnn**. The decision log in `docs/engineering/recommendations.md` remains the summary.

## Consequences
Future readers (including future you) can see why, not just what. Reversing a decision means writing a new ADR, which makes the change deliberate.

# BP-01.04 Subscription and Payments: Business Rules Register (linked to BP-09)

**Source:** *TransitHub — Subscription, Payments and Financial Management Register* (consolidated 26 November 2026; includes decisions approved on 24 November 2026).
**Status of this file:** a faithful record of the source, not analysis. Where analysis documents disagree with an **Approved** item here, this register wins.

**Identifiers:** working references (S1–S13) only. The source refers to the promotion model as FR-SUB-005, so the original requirement IDs exist elsewhere. They should be restored here when available. Do not invent replacements.

**Three financial areas that stay separate (Approved):**

| Area | Who pays whom | Owns |
|---|---|---|
| BP-01.04 Subscription Management | Transport companies pay TransitHub | Plans, subscriptions, promotions, agreements |
| BP-03 Passenger Booking and Ticketing | Passengers pay transport companies | Bookings, tickets, refund workflow |

These areas work together without taking ownership from one another.

## S1. Subscription plan management — Requirements approved
1. Create and manage plans.
2. Define prices.
3. Define features and entitlements.
4. Define company eligibility conditions.
5. Approve and publish plans under control.
6. Change plan terms.
7. **Preserve historical plan versions.**
8. Track which companies subscribe to each plan.
9. Retire or withdraw plans under control.
10. Protect published terms from unauthorised change.

Changing a plan must never silently rewrite an existing company's subscription history.

## S2. Company subscription lifecycle — Requirements approved

| Area | Rule subject |
|---|---|
| Activation | Controlled activation once required conditions are met |
| Commencement | Effective start date recorded |
| Billing period | Applicable period maintained |
| Renewal | Renewal managed |
| Grace period | Eligible late payments handled under approved conditions |
| Changes | Controlled upgrades, downgrades and other changes |
| Cancellation | Authorised requests and decisions recorded |
| Expiry | Subscriptions reaching their end date managed |
| Reactivation | Controlled restoration of an eligible subscription |
| History | Status and transaction history preserved |

- **Subscription status and company operational status are separate concepts.** A subscription problem does not by itself change a company's legal registration status.
- **Open:** grace-period lengths, renewal timing, automatic vs manual renewal, cancellation notice periods, and the consequences of expiry.

## S3. Subscription payment processing — Requirements approved
- Every payment is associated with the right subscription or financial obligation.
- Payment initiation is distinguished from successful payment. **A company is not treated as paid because it initiated a payment.**
- Payment confirmation is verified.
- Every payment has a traceable transaction reference.
- Duplicate payment processing is prevented.
- Failures and uncertain outcomes are recorded.
- Records are reconciled against authoritative transaction information.
- Important payment events are auditable.
- Financial ownership belongs to BP-09.
- Payment confirmation and subscription activation are **separate, controlled events**.

## S4. Payment reconciliation — Requirements approved
1. Compare payment records against authoritative transaction records.
2. Identify payments matching expected obligations.
3. Identify missing or unmatched payments.
4. Detect duplicates.
5. Identify incorrect amounts.
6. Record differences.
7. Assign unresolved differences to authorised personnel.
8. Preserve investigation evidence.
9. Record reconciliation decisions.
10. Keep complete audit history.

**Open:** frequency, matching tolerances, responsible roles and escalation deadlines.

## S5. Subscription refunds and adjustments — Approved 24 November 2026
- Full and partial refunds, starting from a refund request.
- Review **and** approval by authorised finance personnel.
- Record the reason, the amount and a reference to the original payment.
- Process through the original payment method or an approved alternative.
- Update financial records and subscription balances. Preserve refund history.
- **A subscription refund does not automatically cancel the subscription.** Refund and cancellation are separate decisions.
- **Open:** eligibility, time limits, approval thresholds, payment-provider limitations, tax and credit-note treatment, and the effect on subscription periods.

## S6. Payment disputes and exceptions — Requirements approved
- Record disputes and the disputed transactions.
- Assign each dispute to authorised reviewers.
- Preserve evidence and record findings.
- Track outstanding responses.
- Record resolution decisions and keep history.
- Integrate with BP-09.
- **A dispute does not automatically trigger a refund or invalidate a confirmed payment.** It is assessed through an authorised process.

## S7. Promotional campaigns and discounts — Requirements and logical model approved in part
**Scope:**
- Campaigns with start and end dates, pricing and discounts, eligible plans and companies, usage limits, and promotional codes and redemption.
- Referral rewards and partner attribution.
- Discount calculation.
- Campaign approval, performance reporting, alerts and forecasts.
- Historical campaign information.

**Controls:**
1. Codes have controlled eligibility and redemption limits.
2. Duplicate redemption is prevented.
3. Every discount keeps its calculation evidence.
4. The exact offer and eligibility version used is preserved.
5. Failed redemptions remain traceable.
6. Campaign recommendations never automatically change commercial terms.
7. Campaign changes are authorised.
8. Sensitive promotional information is protected.

**Every applied discount preserves:**
- the original subscription price, the promotion applied, the discount amount and the final payable amount;
- the currency and relevant tax information;
- the calculation method and rule version, and the rounding and adjustment evidence;
- the authoritative BP-09 transaction reference.

## S8. Referral rewards — Approved
- Unique referral codes or links, identifying the referring and the referred company.
- Track registrations and verify subscription eligibility.
- Configurable rewards (discounts or credits) with reward limits.
- Detect duplicate and fraudulent referrals.
- **Approval before an eligible reward is applied.** Historical reward records are kept.

**Open:** reward values, eligibility periods and limits.

## S9. Commercial agreements and negotiated subscriptions — Approved
- Authorised agreements may set negotiated prices, special billing terms, discounts and conditions, commencement, expiry and renewal, and amendments.
- Each agreement records party acceptance.
- Changes preserve previous versions and approval history.

## S10. Electronic signing of subscription documents — Approved
1. Identify documents needing signatures.
2. Identify required signatories.
3. Define the signing order.
4. Verify signatory authority.
5. Initiate signing.
6. Track pending, completed, rejected and expired requests.
7. Notify signatories.
8. Preserve signed documents and evidence.
9. Prevent silent modification.
10. Cancel and reissue incorrect requests.
11. Keep a full signing audit trail.

**Open:** provider, identity assurance and legal standards.

## S11. Financial permissions and separation of duties — Approved

| Role | Main responsibility |
|---|---|
| Subscription Officer | Manage plans and subscriptions |
| Finance Officer | Review payments, reconcile, prepare refund requests |
| Authorised Finance Manager | Approve refunds and financial adjustments |
| Platform Administrator | Manage platform-wide settings |

- Job titles alone grant no financial authority. Sensitive actions require explicit permissions.
- Some actions require a second person's approval.
- **The person preparing a refund is not automatically allowed to approve it.**
- Financial actions are auditable. Company financial information is isolated between tenants.

## S12. Subscription knowledge base and support — Requirements approved
- A searchable knowledge base covering plans, add-ons and bundles, payments, invoices, renewals, cancellations, FAQs, troubleshooting and payment problems.
- Articles have version history, authorised publication and links to support requests.
- It supplements human support and does not replace it.

## S13. Audit and investigation management — Requirements approved
- An investigation workspace covering:
  - scope, objectives and review periods;
  - evidence, findings and control weaknesses;
  - corrective actions with owners and deadlines;
  - management responses, conclusions and history.
- Access for auditors is restricted and read-only where possible.

## S15. Overall subscription flow — Proposed (not an approved workflow)
1. The company selects a plan.
2. The agreement is established (standard terms or an authorised commercial agreement).
3. An invoice or payment obligation is created.
4. The company initiates payment.
5. The payment outcome is verified.
6. BP-09 records the authoritative transaction.
7. The subscription is activated or renewed.
8. Billing is managed on an ongoing basis.
